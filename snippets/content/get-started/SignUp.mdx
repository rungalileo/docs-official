## Sign up for Galileo

If you haven't signed up for Galileo do so now.

<Note>
These steps are to sign up for a free Galileo account. If you are in an organization that has a custom Galileo deployment, contact your Galileo administrator to create an account.
</Note>

<Steps>
<Step title="Create an account">
Head to [app.galileo.ai/sign-up](https://app.galileo.ai/sign-up), and create a new account, either using your Google account, or with user name and password.
</Step>
<Step title="Give your organization a name">
Set the name for your organization in Galileo.
</Step>
</Steps>

You will now be logged in to Galileo in your new organization.

<Note>
Each new Galileo organization contains two sample projects. If you want to learn more about these projects, check out our [sample projects guide](/getting-started/sample-projects/sample-projects) to learn more about the sample projects.
</Note>
