Purpose & Instructions
This questionnaire is critical for successful Galileo pre-production and production deployment. Incomplete or incorrect information will delay your deployment and require additional meetings.
The ideal time to fill this out is as early as possible. The fastest we have all the information in this document, the faster we can deploy and add value to your business.
The best point of contact to fill this out is a representative who can either fill out this document in its entirety, or understands how to reach the right points of contact to complete the various items in this document.
Before You Begin:

Required Participants: Involve your Cloud Infrastructure, Security, and DevOps teams to assist with gathering this information
Time Needed: Approximately 45-60 minutes to gather all information


How to Use This Document:
Review each section with the appropriate technical team member
Mark any items as "Unknown - Need Investigation" rather than guessing
Flag any "blockers" that require immediate discussion
Return the completed form with any additional context or constraints

Section 1: Deployment Tier Selection
Understanding Your Options
Your Deployment Tier determines the level of support, monitoring, and deployment complexity. This is the most critical decision for your deployment.

Tier
Description
Benefits
Trade-offs
Best For
Tier 1
Fully Galileo-hosted solution
• Fastest deployment (hours)
• Full monitoring & support
• No infrastructure management
• Automatic updates
• Best SLAs
• Less control
• Data leaves your environment
Organizations prioritizing speed and simplicity
Tier 2
Self-hosted with full Galileo access
• Data stays in your environment
• Strong support capabilities
• Good monitoring coverage
• Longer deployment (days)
• Shared management responsibility
Organizations with data residency requirements but flexible access policies
Tier 3
Self-hosted with limited access
• Full data control
• Compliance-friendly
• Metrics-based monitoring
• Limited support capabilities
• Longer incident resolution
• More customer responsibility
Organizations with strict access controls
Tier 4
Fully isolated self-hosted
• Complete isolation
• Maximum security
• Full compliance control
• External support needed 
• Longest deployment time
Highly regulated industries with strict isolation requirements

Section 6 - Unsupported Configurations

Kubernetes versions below 1.32
Non-Kubernetes container orchestration (e.g., Docker Swarm, Nomad, ECS without EKS)
Deployments via Kubernetes manifests (vs the supported Helm chart deployment method)
Nonstandard Luna base model types
Kubernetes worker nodes that do not support x86 Linux images
IPv6-only networking (IPv4 required, dual-stack acceptable)
Service mesh that breaks standard Kubernetes networking (please specify for evaluation)
Custom CNI that prevents pod-to-pod communication
Self-hosted environments that disallow Galileo’s default Grafana or Clickhouse instances
Nonstandard GPU types (see here for supported types: https://v2docs.galileo.ai/concepts/luna/luna#h100-gpu)
