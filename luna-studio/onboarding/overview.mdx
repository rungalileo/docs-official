The first time you sign in to a fresh org with no projects, Luna Studio routes you to a two-step onboarding wizard. It captures the bare minimum needed to launch your first training run: an LLM provider integration and a project name.

<Frame caption="Onboarding, step 1 — connect at least one LLM provider">
  ![Onboarding step 1: select LLM provider](/images/luna-studio/onboarding/step-1-providers.png)
</Frame>

## When you see onboarding

Luna Studio routes to onboarding when **both** are true:

- Your org has zero projects.
- You haven't dismissed onboarding yet for this org.

If you skip or close it mid-flow, the dismissal is remembered per-org. You can always [create projects](/luna-studio/projects/create-a-project) directly afterwards, and you can [add more integrations](/luna-studio/integrations/overview) any time from the Integrations page.

<Note>
  Today, the dismissal is stored client-side per browser. Once the projects API ships, this will
  move to a server-persisted flag on your org.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Select your LLM provider

The first step asks you to connect at least one LLM provider. Onboarding shows a curated subset of providers — a wider catalog is available later on the [Integrations](/luna-studio/integrations/overview) page.

### Add a provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add integration on a provider card">
    A modal opens with the fields that provider needs.
  </Step>

<Step title="Fill in the credentials">
  For most providers this is a single API key. For Vertex AI, paste your service-account JSON.
</Step>

  <Step title="Click Save changes">
    The card flips to show an **Active** dot, plus **Update** and **Remove** buttons.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [LLM providers](/luna-studio/integrations/llm-providers) for per-provider field reference.

### Remove a provider

Click **Remove** on the card. A confirmation dialog appears — click **Remove** again to confirm.

### Galileo integration

Below the LLM-provider grid is a **Add your Galileo integration** section with a single Galileo card. Adding it now is optional but recommended — it unlocks the "Import from Galileo" dataset source later. See [Galileo integration](/luna-studio/integrations/galileo).

### Continue or skip

The footer button changes based on whether you've added at least one integration:

- **No integrations yet** &mdash; the button reads **Skip** and is shown as an outline button.
- **At least one integration** &mdash; the button reads **Continue** and is filled.

Either path advances to Step 2.

<Tip>
  You don't need to configure all your providers up front. You can add or remove integrations any
  time from the [Integrations page](/luna-studio/integrations/overview) in the sidebar footer.
</Tip>

## Step 2 — Create project

<Frame caption="Onboarding, step 2 — name your first project">
  ![Onboarding step 2: create first project](/images/luna-studio/onboarding/step-2-create-project.png)
</Frame>

The second step has a single field: **Project name**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a project name">
    Use a name that describes what you'll evaluate (e.g. `support-tone-classifier`, `rag-evaluator`).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Continue">
    Luna Studio creates the project, marks onboarding dismissed for this org, and routes you to the project's [Training runs](/luna-studio/projects/training-runs-home) page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also click **Back** to return to Step 1 if you need to adjust your integrations.

## Progress indicator

A small progress bar at the top of the wizard shows how far along you are: 33% on Step 1, 66% on Step 2.

## What you skip with onboarding

Onboarding is a curated path — the full Integrations catalog (12 providers + AWS Bedrock/SageMaker + custom JSON) lives on the [Integrations](/luna-studio/integrations/overview) page. Once onboarding is done, head there to add the rest.
