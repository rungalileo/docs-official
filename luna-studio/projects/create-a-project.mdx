You can create a project two ways: from the **Projects** page (the most common path) or as the second step of [onboarding](/luna-studio/onboarding/overview) on first sign-in.

<Frame caption="Create new project modal">![Create new project modal](/images/luna-studio/projects/new-project-modal.png)</Frame>

## From the Projects page

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Projects page">
  Click **Projects** in the sidebar (or the Luna Studio logo).
</Step>

<Step title="Click New project">The primary button in the top right of the page header. The **Create new project** modal opens.</Step>

<Step title="Enter a project name">
  The field is labelled **Project name** with the placeholder `my-project`.

{" "}

<Tip>Project names are checked for duplicates inline — if you type a name that already exists, an error appears below the field and the **Create** button is disabled until you change it.</Tip>

</Step>

<Step title="Click Create">
  Luna Studio creates the project and routes you to its [Training runs](/luna-studio/projects/training-runs-home) page, ready for your first run.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Naming conventions

There's no strict format, but a few patterns work well:

- **GenAI use case** &mdash; `customer-support-copilot`, `enterprise-search-assistant`.
- **Team or domain + use case** &mdash; `sales-engineering-assistant`, `hr-policy-assistant`.
- **Versioned** &mdash; `customer-support-copilot-v2`, `enterprise-search-assistant-v3` for iterating on the same use case.

Avoid spaces (use hyphens or underscores) and keep names short — they appear in breadcrumbs throughout the app.

## What to do next

Inside the new project, head to the **Training runs** page (you're already there) and click **New run** to launch your first training. The flow is documented in detail in [Creating a new run](/luna-studio/runs/new-run/overview).
