If you can't sign in, you can request a password reset email.

<Frame caption="Forgot password screen">
  ![Forgot password screen](/images/luna-studio/auth/forgot-password.png)
</Frame>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the forgot password page">
    On the [Sign in](/luna-studio/account/sign-in) screen, click **Forgot your password?** inside the password field. Or go directly to [luna-studio.dev.rungalileo.io/forgot-password](https://luna-studio.dev.rungalileo.io/forgot-password).

    The screen reads **Forgot password** with the subtitle "Enter your email address to receive a password reset link".

  </Step>

<Step title="Enter your email">Use the email associated with your Luna Studio account.</Step>

<Step title="Click **Send reset link**">
  If the email matches an account, you'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.
</Step>

  <Step title="Open the email and follow the link">
    The link takes you to a page where you can set a new password. After saving, you're signed in and routed to your org.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Didn't get the email?

- Check your spam folder.
- Confirm the email matches the one on your Luna Studio account.
- Wait a few minutes and try again — high-volume providers occasionally delay delivery.
- Still nothing? See [Troubleshooting](/luna-studio/reference/troubleshooting#cant-sign-in).

## Remember your password?

Click **Sign in** at the bottom of the form to return to the [Sign in](/luna-studio/account/sign-in) screen.
