## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign in page">
    Go to [luna-studio.dev.rungalileo.io/sign-in](https://luna-studio.dev.rungalileo.io/sign-in). The screen reads **Welcome back** with the subtitle "Sign in to access Luna Studio by Galileo".
  </Step>

<Step title="Enter your email and password">
  Both fields are required. If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot your password?**
  inside the password field — see [Reset your password](/luna-studio/account/forgot-password).
</Step>

  <Step title="Click Sign in">
    On success, Luna Studio routes you to your most recent org. If you reached `/sign-in` from a deep link, you're returned to that page after sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Errors

| What you see                  | What it means                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Invalid email or password** | The credentials don't match an active account. Re-check both fields. |

The error appears as a red banner above the form.

## Don't have an account?

Click **Create account** at the bottom of the form to go to [Sign up](/luna-studio/account/sign-up).

## Behind the scenes

<Note>
  When an unauthenticated request hits any Luna Studio page, you're redirected to
  `/sign-in?callbackUrl=...`. After successful sign-in, you're sent back to that callback URL — but
  only if it's on the same origin as Luna Studio. External callback URLs are stripped for security.
</Note>

If you're already signed in and visit `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, or `/forgot-password`, Luna Studio bounces you back to your callback (or your org home).

## Trouble signing in?

- Check that you're using the email you signed up with.
- If the **Invalid email or password** banner appears repeatedly, try [Reset your password](/luna-studio/account/forgot-password).
- Still stuck? See [Troubleshooting](/luna-studio/reference/troubleshooting#cant-sign-in).
