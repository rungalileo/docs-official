Use this page to create a Luna Studio account, sign in to an existing one, or recover access if you forgot your password.

## Create an account

If you don't have a Luna Studio account yet, this is where you start. Sign-up is free.

<Frame caption="Create a Luna Studio account">![Luna Studio sign up screen](/images/luna-studio/auth/sign-up.png)</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign up page">
    Go to Luna Studio and click **Create account** in the top right. The screen reads **Welcome to Luna Studio!** with the subtitle "Get started for free".
  </Step>

{" "}

<Step title="Enter your email and a password">
  Use a work email if you'll be inviting teammates later, and enter a password with at least eight characters.
</Step>

  <Step title="Click Create account">
    You'll be signed in and taken to onboarding. See [Onboarding overview](/luna-studio/onboarding/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

By creating an account you agree to Galileo's [Terms of Service](https://galileo.ai/terms-of-service) and [Privacy Policy](https://galileo.ai/privacy-policy). Both links are also in the sign-up footer.

## Sign in

<Frame caption="Sign in to Luna Studio with your email and password">![Luna Studio sign in screen](/images/luna-studio/auth/sign-in.png)</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign in page">
    Go to Luna Studio and click **Sign in** in the top right. The screen reads **Welcome back** with the subtitle "Sign in to access Luna Studio by Galileo".
  </Step>

{" "}

<Step title="Enter your email and password">Both fields are required. If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot your password?** inside the password field and continue to [Reset your password](#reset-your-password).</Step>

  <Step title="Click Sign in">
    On success, Luna Studio routes you to your most recent org. If you reached `/sign-in` from a deep link, you're returned to that page after sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Errors

| What you see                  | What it means                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Invalid email or password** | The credentials don't match an active account. Re-check both fields. |

The error appears as a red banner above the form.

<Note>
  When an unauthenticated request hits any Luna Studio page, you're redirected to `/sign-in?callbackUrl=...`. After successful sign-in, you're sent back to that callback URL - but only if it's on the same origin as Luna Studio. External
  callback URLs are stripped for security.
</Note>

If you're already signed in and visit `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, or `/forgot-password`, Luna Studio bounces you back to your callback (or your org home).

Still stuck? See [Troubleshooting](/luna-studio/reference/troubleshooting#cant-sign-in).

## Reset your password

If you can't sign in, you can request a password reset email.

<Frame caption="Forgot password screen">![Forgot password screen](/images/luna-studio/auth/forgot-password.png)</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the forgot password page">On the sign in screen, click **Forgot your password?** inside the password field. Or go directly to Luna Studio and click **Forgot your password?** in the sign in form. The screen reads **Forgot password** with a subtitle asking for your email address.</Step>

{" "}

<Step title="Enter your email">Use the email associated with your Luna Studio account.</Step>

{" "}

<Step title="Click Send reset link">If the email matches an account, you'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.</Step>

  <Step title="Open the email and follow the link">
    The link takes you to a page where you can set a new password. After saving, you're signed in and routed to your org.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Didn't get the email?

- Check your spam folder.
- Confirm the email matches the one on your Luna Studio account.
- Wait a few minutes and try again - high-volume providers occasionally delay delivery.
- Still nothing? See [Troubleshooting](/luna-studio/reference/troubleshooting#cant-sign-in).

## What happens next

On first sign-in, Luna Studio routes you to the **onboarding** page:

1. Pick a supported LLM provider integration.
2. Create your first project.

After onboarding, you land on the project's **Training runs** page - ready to launch your first run.

<Note>Onboarding is shown only the first time you visit your org with no projects. If you skip or close it mid-flow, Luna Studio remembers the dismissal per-org so you don't see it again on the next visit.</Note>
