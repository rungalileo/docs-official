If you don't have a Luna Studio account yet, this is where you start. Sign-up is free.

<Frame caption="Create a Luna Studio account">
  ![Luna Studio sign up screen](/images/luna-studio/auth/sign-up.png)
</Frame>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign up page">
    Go to [luna-studio.dev.rungalileo.io/sign-up](https://luna-studio.dev.rungalileo.io/sign-up). The screen reads **Welcome to Luna Studio!** with the subtitle "Get started for free".
  </Step>

<Step title="Enter your email and a password">
  - **Email** &mdash; Use a work email if you'll be inviting teammates later. - **Password** &mdash;
  Enter 8 or more characters.
</Step>

  <Step title="Click Create account">
    You'll be signed in and taken to onboarding. See [Onboarding overview](/luna-studio/onboarding/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Already have an account?

Click **Sign in** at the bottom of the form to go to [Sign in](/luna-studio/account/sign-in).

## Legal

By creating an account you agree to Galileo's [Terms of Service](https://galileo.ai/terms-of-service) and [Privacy Policy](https://galileo.ai/privacy-policy). Both links are also in the sign-up footer.

## What happens next

On first sign-in, Luna Studio routes you to the **onboarding** wizard:

1. Pick an LLM provider integration (OpenAI, Vertex AI, or Anthropic).
2. Create your first project.

After onboarding, you land on the project's **Training runs** page — ready to launch your first run.

<Note>
  Onboarding is shown only the first time you visit your org with no projects. If you skip or close
  it mid-flow, Luna Studio remembers the dismissal per-org so you don't see it again on the next
  visit.
</Note>
