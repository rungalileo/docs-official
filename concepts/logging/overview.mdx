## What is AI Observability

Because agentic applications are non-deterministic, they require specialized observability. AI observability gives your organization visibility into how your AI applications behave in production. Once instrumented, Galileo captures every session, trace, and span, generating real-time data that helps you understand workflows, diagnose problems, and improve application performance.

## Core concepts

- [Log streams](/sdk-api/logging/logging-basics) and [projects](/concepts/projects) organize the data you send to Galileo for a given project or environment.
- [Sessions](/concepts/logging/sessions/sessions-overview) group related traces into one end-to-end interaction or run.
- [Traces](/sdk-api/logging/galileo-logger#start-a-trace) represent a complete AI workflow or request.
- [Spans](/sdk-api/logging/galileo-logger#add-spans) capture the individual steps within a trace, such as model calls or tool calls.
