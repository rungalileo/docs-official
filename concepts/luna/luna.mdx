## Overview

LLMs are powerful judges for evaluations, but as your application scales up to from tens or hundreds of traces a day to thousands or millions, they can fall short.

- LLMs are expensive
- LLMs don't provide the performance needed, especially for realtime guardrails
- LLMs are general purpose, and even leveraging [CLHF](/concepts/metrics/custom-metrics/continuous-learning-via-human-feedback) to enhance the evaluation prompts, can still be less effective for your specific needs.

The Luna 2 model mitigates these issues:

- Being an SLM, it is an **order of magnitude cheaper** to run than most LLMs
- SLMs run an **order of magnitude faster**, allowing for **real-time guardrails**
- Luna is not only **fine-tuned for evaluations**, giving comparable performance out of the box with the top LLMs, but it can be **further fine-tuned using your data** to improve accuracy beyond any general purpose LLM.

The Luna 2 model works with most of the [out of the box metrics](/concepts/metrics/metrics-reference#luna-metrics), or your [LLM as a judge custom metrics](http://localhost:3000/concepts/metrics/custom-metrics/custom-metrics-ui-llm).
