<Note>
  The AI Assistant is in **Beta**. Responses are AI-generated and can be incomplete or incorrect — verify anything you act on. The feature set is evolving, and behavior may change.
</Note>

The AI Assistant is a context-aware chat companion built into the Galileo console. It knows which Log stream, experiment, metric, or session you are looking at, pulls the relevant data for you, and answers questions like *"Why is this metric failing?"* and *"What should I change to fix it?"* — with citations and links back to the exact traces it used.

It is the **pull-based** half of Galileo's investigation experience. Signals proactively pushes the unknown unknowns to you; the Assistant lets you ask follow-up questions and investigate on demand. The two work together: a Signal can surface a problem, and you can open the Assistant to dig into the *why* and the *how to fix*.

## Why use it

Traces tell you *what* happened. The Assistant helps you understand *why* it happened and *what to do next* — in seconds, in plain language.

## What you can ask

The Assistant is most useful when your question is grounded in data Galileo already has. Group your questions by what you are trying to do:

| Goal | Example questions |
|------|-------------------|
| **Root cause a failure** | "Why is *Action Completion* false across these sessions?" · "Why did this agent fail to finish the task?" |
| **Understand a metric** | "What does *Context Adherence* measure?" · "Why is this trace scoring low on *Tool Selection Quality*?" |
| **Reconcile disagreeing metrics** | "Why is *Correctness* true but *Completeness* false on this response?" |
| **Get a fix** | "What should I change to improve this metric?" · "How do I stop this hallucination?" |
| **Validate agent behavior** | "Which tools should this metric evaluate based on these traces?" |
| **Create an eval** | "Recommend a metric for this failure" — then follow the link to a pre-filled metric creation page. |

<Tip>
  Anchor your question to a specific Log stream, experiment, metric, or session. The more context the Assistant has about *where* you are, the sharper the answer.
</Tip>

## Where to find it

The Assistant opens as a side panel in the console. It pushes the page content aside rather than covering it, so you can keep your traces and the conversation on screen at once. The panel stays open as you navigate between pages.

Open the Assistant from a Log stream or experiment to ask aggregate questions across everything in view — for example, the top reasons a metric is failing.

When you open a fresh conversation, the Assistant suggests a few starting questions based on what you are viewing.

## Conversations

Every conversation is saved so you can return to an investigation later.

- **History** — browse, search, rename, and delete past conversations.
- **Citations** — answers include inline links to the traces, sessions, and docs the Assistant used, so you can verify the reasoning.
- **Context indicator** — a meter shows how much of the conversation's context window is in use, with a warning at 90% so you know when to start a fresh thread.

## Scope and limitations

The Assistant is in Beta. Today it can:

- Answer questions grounded in **Log streams (Logs) and Experiments**.
- Explain metrics, root-cause failures, and recommend fixes.
- Surface relevant Signals inside the chat panel.
- Link you to a pre-filled creation page when it recommends a new metric.

It does **not** yet:

- Answer questions about datasets, playgrounds, or standalone metric views.
- Take actions on your behalf — creating evals or controls directly from chat is on the roadmap.
- Replace your own judgment. Responses are AI-generated; confirm anything before you ship a change based on it.

<Note>
  The Assistant uses your configured [LLM integration](/getting-started/evaluate-and-improve/evaluate-and-improve#configure-an-llm-integration) to generate answers. Make sure an integration is set up for your project.
</Note>

## Tips for better answers

- **Be specific about the symptom.** "Why is *Action Completion* false on these 12 sessions?" beats "what's wrong with my agent?"
- **Start from the right level.** Use cell level for a single bad result, page level for patterns across a stream.
- **Follow the citations.** When an answer matters, click through to the source traces to confirm it.
- **Iterate in one thread.** Ask a follow-up rather than restarting — the Assistant keeps the context of your investigation.
