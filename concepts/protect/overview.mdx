Galileo Protect provides real-time guardrails for your AI applications, checking inputs to and outputs from LLMs against a range of metrics, allowing you to block user actions, or respond differently to the user.

<Note>
Protect either requires Luna-2 running on th enterprise tier of Galileo, or custom code based metrics. [Contact us](https://galileo.ai/contact-sales) to get started with Luna-2.
</Note>
