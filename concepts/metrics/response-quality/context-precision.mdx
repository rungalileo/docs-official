<DefinitionCard>
  <strong>Context Precision</strong> measures the percentage of retrieved chunks that are relevant to the user's query, weighted by their position in the retrieval order.
</DefinitionCard>

Context Precision is a continuous metric ranging from 0 to 1:

<Scale
  low="0"
  lowLabel="Low Precision"
  high="1"
  highLabel="High Precision"
  lowDescription="Few or no retrieved chunks are relevant to the query"
  highDescription="Most or all retrieved chunks are relevant to the query"
/>

This metric helps you evaluate the quality of your retrieval system by measuring how many of the retrieved chunks actually contain useful information for answering the query. Higher Context Precision indicates that your retrieval system is successfully identifying and returning relevant content.

## Calculation method

Context Precision is computed as a weighted average of [Chunk Relevance](/concepts/metrics/response-quality/chunk-relevance) scores, where chunks at higher positions (earlier in the retrieval order) are given more weight:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Chunk Relevance Calculation">
    First, [Chunk Relevance](/concepts/metrics/response-quality/chunk-relevance) is computed for each retrieved chunk, producing a binary classification (Relevant or Not Relevant) for each chunk.

</Step>
  <Step title="Position Weighting">
    Each chunk's relevance is weighted by its position in the retrieval order, with earlier positions receiving higher weights (using a 1/(i+1) weighting scheme where i is the position index).

</Step>
  <Step title="Weighted Sum Calculation">
    The numerator is calculated as the sum of weighted relevance scores: for each relevant chunk at position i, add 1/(i+1) to the sum.

</Step>
  <Step title="Normalization">
    The denominator is the sum of all position weights (1/(i+1) for all positions), ensuring the metric ranges from 0 to 1.

</Step>
  <Step title="Final Score">
    Context Precision is computed as the ratio of the weighted sum of relevant chunks to the total weighted sum of all positions.

</Step>
</Steps>

The position weighting ensures that relevant chunks appearing earlier in the retrieval order contribute more to the final score, reflecting the importance of ranking quality in retrieval systems.

<Note type="info">
  Context Precision is differentiated from [Precision @ K](/concepts/metrics/response-quality/precision-at-k): Context Precision uses position weighting and considers all retrieved chunks, while Precision @ K evaluates precision at a specific rank K without weighting.
</Note>

When Context Precision scores are low, it indicates that many retrieved chunks are not relevant to the query. To improve your system:

<div style={{ marginTop: "1rem", paddingTop: "0.75rem", borderTop: "1px solid rgba(209, 213, 219, 0.33)" }}>
  <strong>Improve retrieval quality:</strong> Refine your embedding models, similarity search algorithms, or retrieval parameters to better match queries with relevant content.
</div>

<div style={{ marginTop: "0.75rem", paddingTop: "0.75rem", borderTop: "1px solid rgba(209, 213, 219, 0.33)" }}>
  <strong>Implement reranking:</strong> Use a reranking model to improve the order of retrieved chunks, ensuring the most relevant ones appear first.
</div>

<div style={{ marginTop: "0.75rem", paddingTop: "0.75rem", borderTop: "1px solid rgba(209, 213, 219, 0.33)" }}>
  <strong>Adjust Top K:</strong> If precision is consistently low, consider reducing the number of chunks retrieved (Top K) to focus on higher-quality results.
</div>

<div style={{ marginTop: "0.75rem", paddingTop: "0.75rem", borderTop: "1px solid rgba(209, 213, 219, 0.33)" }}>
  <strong>Analyze retrieval patterns:</strong> Examine which types of queries or content lead to low precision scores to identify systematic issues.
</div>

## Best practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Use for Retrieval Assessment" icon="chart-line">
    Leverage Context Precision when evaluating the overall quality of your retrieval system's results and determining how accurately it adheres to queries.

</Card>
  <Card title="Combine with Precision @ K" icon="link">
    Use Context Precision alongside [Precision @ K](/concepts/metrics/response-quality/precision-at-k) to understand both overall precision and precision at specific ranks.

</Card>
  <Card title="Monitor Ranking Quality" icon="arrow-up-arrow-down">
    Track Context Precision to ensure your retrieval system not only finds relevant chunks but also ranks them appropriately.

</Card>
  <Card title="Optimize Retrieval Parameters" icon="sliders">
    Use Context Precision scores to guide adjustments to your Top K value, similarity thresholds, and reranking strategies.

</Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
When optimizing for Context Precision, remember that the position weighting means that improving the ranking of relevant chunks (moving them earlier in the retrieval order) will have a significant positive impact on your score, even if the total number of relevant chunks remains the same.

</Note>
