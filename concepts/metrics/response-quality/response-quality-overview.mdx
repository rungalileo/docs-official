Response quality metrics help you measure how well your AI system answers user questions, follows instructions, and provides useful information in any setting — with or without RAG.

Use these metrics when you want to:

- Ensure responses are **factually correct**.
- Check that the model **follows instructions** in your prompts.
- Evaluate responses against **known correct answers** (ground truth).

The core Response Quality metrics are:

- **Ground Truth Adherence** – Measures whether the response is semantically equivalent to your reference answer (experiments only).
- **Correctness** – Measures whether the response contains factually accurate information.
- **Instruction Adherence** – Measures whether the model followed the instructions in your prompt template.
